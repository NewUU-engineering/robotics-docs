# Training plan

## Practise independently

Work through this list before the next session. Start with the emergency stop and drill it until it is automatic.

- [ ] **Emergency stop ++l2+b++** — drill this first, to the point of reflex
- [ ] Soft stop with a single press of START
- [ ] Power-on and power-off to procedure, including the ≥2 s hold and the one-minute wait
- [ ] The full startup chain: `Zero Torque → L2+B → L2+↑ → R2+A`
- [ ] Posture transitions: ++l2+a++ squat, `L2+←` seated, ++l2+x++ lying and standing
- [ ] Standing the robot up from the floor
- [ ] Walking, turning, and switching speeds (2× ++l2++ / 2× ++l1++)
- [ ] Drift compensation from the remote (`R1 + arrows`)
- [ ] Interactive gestures (++select+a++ / `+Y` / `+X`)
- [ ] Dance mode with the required clear space
- [ ] Connecting the application in AP mode
- [ ] Demo Teaching — record and replay your own gesture
- [ ] Building a LiDAR map and a patrol route
- [ ] Mounting the robot on the gantry and removing it

## Requires an instructor present

These are not to be attempted without an instructor:

- Full calibration
- Low-level joint control
- Any work that involves opening the body

## Session structure for instructors

1. Run the [pre-session checklist](workspace.md#pre-session-checklist) before students enter the room.
2. Brief every new group on [how to stop the robot](safety.md#how-to-stop-the-robot) before the robot is powered on.
3. Keep the robot on the gantry for anything beyond basic walking and gestures.
4. Clear 3 m in every direction before Dance mode, and confirm nobody stands in front of the robot.
5. Shut down to procedure: squat or suspend, confirm Damping, then power off.
